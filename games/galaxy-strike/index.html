The Iron Dominion is building a planet-killer. Fly the Starhawk and stop them!


How to play • Drag your finger anywhere to fly. On a computer use the arrow keys.
• Your lasers fire by themselves. Put the circle on the enemy.
• When a target box turns red (LOCK), tap TORPEDO. It chases them down!
• Hear beeping and see ⚠ TORPEDO? Move fast to dodge. Watch out for asteroids!
• Fly through glowing pickups: 🔧 shields, 🚀 torpedoes, ⭐ more lasers.
• Beat the boss to finish each mission and earn up to 3 ★.
