PIXELWRESTLING

A Max's World game

How to play
Win by pinning your opponent for a 1-2-3 count!
• PUNCH and KICK to knock them down. 3 hits in a row = knockdown.
• GRAB when you're close for a body slam.
• JUMP then KICK in the air = dropkick!
• When they're down, stand close and press PIN (the grab button).
• Walk into a corner and press JUMP to climb. Press JUMP again to fly!
• Gold bar full? GRAB does a MEGA SLAM.
• Getting pinned? Tap any button fast to kick out!
