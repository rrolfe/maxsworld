FOOTBALL

Run it. Throw it. Dive for the end zone. Celebrate!



How to play • Pick a play, then tap HIKE.
• Drag on the left side of the screen to run. On a computer use the arrow keys.
• Passing: hold 1, 2 or 3 and watch the power bar. Let go when it's in the green zone to hit your receiver. The yellow ✕ on the field shows where the ball will land. Your receiver runs to the ball for you, and if he's open he always catches it!
• Hold on to the ball: there's a small chance of a fumble when you get tackled.
• Your game saves after every play. Leave anytime and tap CONTINUE GAME to pick up where you left off.
• With the ball: DIVE to reach for extra yards or into the end zone. JUKE makes a tackler miss. Near the goal line, STRUT high-steps in.
• On defense you control the player with the yellow arrow. DIVE to tackle, SWITCH to change players.
• Score a touchdown and pick your celebration!
• College mode: beat every college to fill your trophy case.
