SKY STRIKE
CALLSIGN: RILEY

Fly Riley One into battle. 5 missions. 5 giant bosses.


How to play • Drag your finger anywhere to fly. On a computer use the arrow keys.
• Your guns fire by themselves. Line up the green circle with the enemy.
• When a target box turns red (LOCK), tap MISSILE. It chases them down!
• When you hear the beeping and see MISSILE!, move fast to dodge.
• Fly through glowing pickups: 🔧 repairs, 🚀 more missiles, ⭐ bigger guns.
• Beat the boss to finish each mission and earn up to 3 ★.
