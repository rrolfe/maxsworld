SKY HERO

6 levels of flying fun · A Max's World game


How to play
• Hold ▲ and ▼ to fly up and down.
• BOOST makes you go faster.
• The big green button changes each level:
  TRICK = do a barrel roll. You can't get hit while rolling!
  DROP = drop a box. Land it on the X!
  FIRE = shoot the boss blimp.
• You have 3 hearts. Birds, rocks and lightning take one away.
• Collect stuff to earn up to 3 ★ per level.
• Stars unlock new planes in the Hangar!
